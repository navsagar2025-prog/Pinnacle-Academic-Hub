JEE Main 2023 — Synthetic Sample Paper (for parser self-test)

1. The SI unit of force is
(1) joule
(2) newton
(3) watt
(4) pascal

2. The acceleration due to gravity on the surface of the Earth is approximately
(1) 5.6 m/s^2
(2) 9.8 m/s^2
(3) 11.2 m/s^2
(4) 19.6 m/s^2

3. Which of the following is a vector quantity?
(1) mass
(2) speed
(3) velocity
(4) energy

4. A body of mass 2 kg is acted upon by a net force of 10 N. Its acceleration is
(1) 0.2 m/s^2
(2) 5 m/s^2
(3) 12 m/s^2
(4) 20 m/s^2

5. The work done by gravity on a body falling freely is
(1) zero
(2) negative
(3) positive
(4) cannot be determined

Answer Key
1. (2)
2. (2)
3. (3)
4. (2)
5. (3)
